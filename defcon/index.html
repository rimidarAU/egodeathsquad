About

A simulated match in the style of Introversion's DEFCON, on 1980s Cold War assumptions.

North America, Europe, and Australia represent NATO. Russia and Asia represent the Warsaw Pact.

Latin America usually tends to the Warsaw Pact, but can be persuaded with good diplomacy. Africa has similar sentiments, but usually abides by the Non-Aligned Movement. Of course, they may have their own plans..

Score: +2 per million killed, −1 per million of your own lost. One match every hour, generated from the clock, so everyone watching sees the same one.
