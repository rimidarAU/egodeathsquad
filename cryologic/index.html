01  What it isThe fourth zone

Inside the
ship itself.

Marathon's other three zones are on the colony world below. Cryo Archive is different. It sits aboard the UESC Marathon, the derelict colony ship the game is named after, and it is the game's endgame zone.

Bungie frames it as a raid-style PvPvE map. You still drop in with your crew, fight other crews and try to extract, but the run is built around puzzles, heavily defended UESC security, and locked vaults that hold the best loot in the game.

It plays more linearly than the other zones. Reports describe long, tight corridors that suddenly open into huge chambers, so fights swing between close quarters and open ground.

02  Getting inNot open to everyone

Earn the
door.

Cryo Archive is gated. Two requirements are reported consistently: reach level 25 in the current season, and establish a connection with all six factions. CyberAcme is available from the start, so in practice that means unlocking the other five.

One outlet also reported a minimum loadout value of 5,000 credits. Other guides do not mention it, so treat it as unconfirmed.

Availability has changed over time. At launch the zone was open on weekends only. In the run-up to Season 2 it opened every day, and Ranked Cryo Archive ran as a separate event that did not overlap with the regular queue.

This page reflects reporting from March to July 2026. Opening times and requirements can change each season, so check Bungie's own Marathon news before you plan a run.

03  InsideClearance, vaults, and what waits at the end

Climb the
clearance.

Security clearance

Progress inside the zone runs on a security clearance system. Higher-tier areas, better exfil points and certain rooms stay locked until your crew raises its clearance, which you do by pushing through the map.

Vaults and keys

The zone holds a series of vaults. Each has its own entry challenge and needs its own vault key. You do not need a key to enter Cryo Archive itself; keys open specific vaults and locked doors once you are inside.

The seventh vault

Bungie has said the seventh vault holds a major boss, described as something even the UESC is afraid of. It is the zone's final encounter.

Progress on Cryo Archive subroutines carried over into Season 2 rather than resetting.

04  How it was unlockedA community puzzle

The players
opened it.

Cryo Archive was not simply switched on. After launch, a cryptic in-game message set the whole player base a goal: kill 500 million UESC between them. That was followed by an alternate-reality puzzle in the style of Bungie's Destiny mysteries.

The community worked through it together, and the solution revealed the zone's entry requirements and its release date.

Why it matters

The unlock set the tone for the zone: Cryo Archive is the part of Marathon where the game's mysteries live, and where its most dedicated crews spend their weekends.
