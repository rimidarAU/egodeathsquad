01  Cosmic censorshipThe decency hypothesis

The horizon
is a courtesy.

In an ordinary collapse the horizon forms first. The singularity arrives afterwards, already wrapped, and no signal from it can reach anyone outside. Whatever the equations do at infinite density, the universe is spared the sight. Penrose named this hope cosmic censorship: every singularity comes dressed.

It is a conjecture, not a theorem. Nobody has proved that nature obeys it. Spin a black hole too fast, or load it with too much charge, and the formula for the horizon radius asks for the square root of a negative number. There is no horizon to form. The thing at the centre is simply there, in plain view, like an open wound in the sky.

Physics does not break at a naked singularity. Physics continues. That is the problem: it continues from a point where it cannot tell you what happens next.

Redaction holding. Redaction holding. Redaction leaking.

03  Beyond the Cauchy horizon03  The end of predictionThird Mystery

The equations
stop answering.

4Relativity is deterministic. Give it the universe at one instant and it hands you every instant after. That promise holds up to a surface called the Cauchy horizon. Past it, the future is no longer fixed by the past. A naked singularity puts that surface in the open, so the region where the future is unwritten includes here.

5Anything consistent with the equations may emerge from the point. Not probably. Not eventually. Simply: may. Light. Matter. Field configurations that have never existed. The mathematics does not forbid it, because the mathematics has nothing to say. You are standing in a part of spacetime that the laws have declined to describe. You are standing in a part of spacetime. You are standing. And the heaven departed as a scroll when it is rolled together.6:14

6The horror of a black hole is that you cannot know what is inside. The horror of a naked singularity is that you can, and it does not know either. ⛧
